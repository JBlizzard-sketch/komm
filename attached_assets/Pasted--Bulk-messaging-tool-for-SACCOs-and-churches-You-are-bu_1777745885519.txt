 Bulk messaging tool for SACCOs and churches


You are building a full production-grade bulk communication platform — SMS, WhatsApp, and email — designed specifically for Kenyan SACCOs, churches, and member-based organisations. Read the full brief, expand the vision, present ~20 substantial phases, wait for "next."
The pain:
Kenya has over 5,000 registered SACCOs and tens of thousands of churches, each with hundreds to thousands of members who need to receive regular communications — contribution reminders, meeting notices, AGM dates, loan approval alerts, event announcements, emergency notifications. The current tools are either expensive SMS bureau services with terrible interfaces, manual WhatsApp broadcasts (limited to 256 contacts and completely untracked), or nothing at all. Generic bulk messaging tools are designed for marketing teams, not for a SACCO treasurer or a church administrator — they are complex, expensive, and don't understand the Kenyan context.
What you are building:
A dead-simple bulk messaging dashboard. The administrator uploads a contacts list (or syncs their member database), types their message, selects the channel (SMS via Africa's Talking, WhatsApp via Cloud API, or email), and sends. The platform shows delivery reports, open rates where available, and failed numbers. For SACCOs, there are pre-built message templates: contribution reminder, loan repayment alert, meeting notice, dividend announcement. For churches: Sunday service reminder, tithe acknowledgement, event invite, prayer request. Members can reply and the administrator sees all replies in one inbox.
Critical nuances:
Africa's Talking SMS API is the right backbone for SMS in Kenya — cheaper than Twilio, better local delivery. WhatsApp message templates must be pre-approved by Meta — the platform should handle this process for the customer, pre-loading all the approved templates they need. The contact list upload must handle messy Excel files — duplicate numbers, formatting inconsistencies, mixed Safaricom/Airtel/Telkom numbers. SACCO contribution reminder messages must be personalisable — "Dear John, your contribution of Ksh 5,000 is due on Friday" — which requires mail-merge style variable substitution. Pricing at Ksh 2,000–4,000/mo plus message cost pass-through is the right model.
Expand this. Think about: scheduled messages (automatically send the Friday contribution reminder every week); two-way SMS for member replies; a member self-service portal where they can check their contribution history; integration with SACCO management software (M-Changa, Coop Bank SACCO systems); a donation/tithe collection link embedded in church messages; an automated birthday message feature; and a reporting module that shows communication engagement over time.
Hosting: Next.js on Vercel. Backend on Railway. Africa's Talking and WhatsApp Cloud API integrations. Background job queue for large sends (5,000 messages should not block the UI). PostgreSQL. Docker-ready for volume.
